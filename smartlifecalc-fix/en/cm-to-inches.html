Centimeters and inches in everyday life

Since 1959 one inch has been defined as exactly 2.54 centimeters, so the conversion never changes — only the rounding does. This converter shows inches, feet and inches, and the nearest 1/16 inch for tape-measure work.

The formula

inches = cm ÷ 2.54  |  cm = inches × 2.54

Worked examples

  • Height: 170 cm ÷ 2.54 = 66.93 in = 5 ft 6.9 in.
  • The other way: 5 ft 10 in = 70 in × 2.54 = 177.8 cm.
  • A 55-inch TV measures 55 × 2.54 = 139.7 cm diagonally (the screen, not the frame).
  • Jeans with a 32-inch waist are 81.3 cm around.

Paper and photo sizes: A-series versus U.S. inches

Most of the world prints on A4; the United States uses Letter. They are close enough to confuse and different enough to cut off a margin, and photo frames sold in centimeters have the same problem.

Quick mental math

  • 10 cm is a little under 4 inches (3.94 in).
  • 30 cm is about one foot (11.8 in).
  • For a rough cm → in estimate, multiply by 4 and divide by 10: 50 cm ≈ 20 in (exact: 19.7 in).

Common mistakes

  • Writing 5.6 ft for 5 ft 6 in — 5.6 ft is 5 ft 7.2 in.
  • Rounding too early: convert first, then round at the end.
  • Measuring screens across the frame: TV and monitor sizes are the visible diagonal only.

When people need this conversion

Online shopping is the most common reason: furniture, rugs, luggage and clothing from other countries often list only centimeters or only inches. Doctors’ offices, passports and sports profiles may ask for height in a different unit than you are used to.

For anything that must fit — a sofa through a doorway, a TV in a cabinet, a carry-on in an overhead bin — convert the exact measurement and leave a little extra room. Product dimensions are usually rounded, and packaging adds size.

Moving-day checklist for metric furniture

Common U.S. interior doors are 30 or 32 inches wide (76.2 or 81.3 cm); exterior doors are usually 36 inches (91.4 cm). Suppose a sofa from a European retailer is listed as 220 x 90 x 78 cm.

  1. Convert every dimension: 86.6 x 35.4 x 30.7 inches.
  2. Compare the two smallest dimensions with the door. The 35.4-inch depth will not pass a 32-inch door upright, but tipped on its side the 30.7-inch height clears with 1.3 inches to spare.
  3. Measure the clear opening with the door open, not the door slab; the frame, stops and hinges take back an inch or more.
  4. Walk the route: hallway width, stair landings and the elevator door.
  5. Allow for packaging. Shipping cartons add several centimeters, so unbox at the door if the margin is thin.
  6. Check whether the legs unscrew; removing them reduces the height dimension that matters when the sofa goes through sideways.
