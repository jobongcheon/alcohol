Counting days between dates the right way

"How many days until…" sounds simple, but calendars have months of 28 to 31 days and leap years every four years (with exceptions). This calculator counts actual calendar days, and can also add or subtract days from a date — useful for deadlines, notice periods, travel and countdowns.

How to use the date calculator

  1. Choose “Days between two dates” or “Add or subtract days”.
  2. Pick the dates (today is pre-selected).
  3. Press Calculate.

The formula

Days = end date − start date (add 1 to include both days)

Worked examples

  • From January 1, 2026 to December 25, 2026 is 358 days.
  • 90 days after November 14, 2026 is February 12, 2027.
  • 30 days before March 1, 2027 is January 30, 2027 (February 2027 has 28 days).

Federal holidays that stretch a business-day count

The weekday count above skips Saturdays and Sundays but not holidays, because holidays differ by state, employer and bank. For federal deadlines and most banks, subtract these 11 federal holidays when they fall inside your range:

  • New Year's Day (Jan 1), Martin Luther King Jr. Day (third Monday in January), Washington's Birthday (third Monday in February)
  • Memorial Day (last Monday in May), Juneteenth (June 19), Independence Day (July 4)
  • Labor Day (first Monday in September), Columbus Day (second Monday in October), Veterans Day (Nov 11)
  • Thanksgiving (fourth Thursday in November), Christmas Day (Dec 25)

A fixed-date holiday that falls on Saturday is observed on Friday; one that falls on Sunday moves to Monday. Example: 10 business days from Friday, November 20, 2026 is Monday, December 7, because Thanksgiving on November 26 drops out. Fourteen calendar days would end on December 4. For federal tax filings, a due date that lands on a weekend or legal holiday moves to the next business day.

Inclusive or exclusive?

Most calculators, including this one, don't count the start date: from Monday to Tuesday is 1 day. If you need to count both ends — for example, a hotel stay counted in days instead of nights, or "a 7-day course from the 1st to the 7th" — add 1 to the result.

Calendar days vs business days

Many legal and banking deadlines use business days, which skip weekends and public holidays. A "10 business day" refund can take two full weeks or more. Count calendar days here, then subtract weekends and holidays if your deadline uses business days.

Leap years

A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4, except century years, which must be divisible by 400. So 2028 is a leap year, 2100 is not, and 2000 was. The calculator includes February 29 automatically.

Two things to double-check

  • Use “include the end date” when counting both the first and last day, like a hotel stay versus nights.
  • Weekdays exclude Saturdays and Sundays but not public holidays.

90 days and three months are not the same

Contracts, leases and medical follow-ups mix the two. Adding three months keeps the same day of the month (clamped to the last day if the month is shorter), while 90 days is a fixed count. The results can differ by a day or more:

If a document says "90 days", use the add-days mode; if it says "three months", count on the calendar. When money or rights depend on it, ask which one the other party means and get it in writing.

Date questions we hear most

Is "3 months" the same as 90 days?
No. Three calendar months can be 89 to 92 days. Check which your contract uses.

How do I count business days?
The result shows weekdays (Monday to Friday). Subtract any public holidays in that range.

What date is 100 days from today?
Choose “Add or subtract days”, keep today as the start date and enter 100.
