Comparing hourly pay and salaries fairly

An hourly wage and a yearly salary only compare fairly when you use the same hours. This calculator turns either one into hourly, weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly, monthly and yearly figures.

How to use the hourly to salary calculator

  1. Choose hourly → salary or salary → hourly.
  2. Enter the amount, hours per week and weeks per year.
  3. Press Convert.

The formula

Yearly salary = hourly wage × hours per week × weeks per year

Worked examples

  • $25 an hour, 40 hours, 52 weeks = $52,000 a year — $4,333 a month, $2,000 every two weeks, or $2,167 twice a month.
  • A $85,000 salary at 40 hours × 52 weeks (2,080 hours) = $40.87 an hour.
  • If the same $25-an-hour job gives two unpaid weeks off, use 50 weeks: $50,000.

Case study: two offers, same job, very different hourly rates

Offer A is a $62,000 salary. The manager says a typical week is 45 hours. Offer B is $31 an hour, non-exempt, scheduled for 40 hours.

  • Offer A at 45 hours × 52 weeks = 2,340 hours, so $62,000 ÷ 2,340 = $26.50 an hour. Only at a true 40-hour week does it reach $29.81.
  • Offer B at 40 hours is $31 × 2,080 = $64,480 a year. If the job really runs 45 hours, the extra 5 are paid at time and a half ($46.50), making $1,472.50 a week or $76,570 a year.

Now add benefits. If Offer A includes employer health coverage worth $500 a month and Offer B has none, A is worth about $68,000 against B's $64,480 at 40 hours. The moment B's overtime becomes routine, B pulls ahead again. The decision rule: convert every offer to dollars per hour actually worked, then add benefits as a yearly dollar figure, never as a vague percentage.

What the numbers leave out

  • Overtime: under federal law, non-exempt workers earn 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a week.
  • Benefits: health insurance, retirement matches and paid leave can be worth 20–30% on top of a salary.
  • Taxes: all figures are gross pay, before income tax, Social Security and Medicare.

Common mistakes

  • Using 4 weeks for a month: a month averages 4.33 weeks, so weekly pay × 4 understates monthly income.
  • Comparing a 37.5-hour job to a 40-hour job without changing the hours field.

Using it to compare job offers

When one offer is hourly and another is salaried, convert both to the same unit and include the expected hours. A salaried job that regularly needs 50 hours a week pays less per hour than the same salary at 40 hours.

Then add the value of benefits and commuting costs. A slightly lower salary with employer health insurance and a retirement match can be worth more than a higher hourly wage without them.

Salaried does not mean exempt from overtime

Federal law requires overtime at 1.5 times the regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek for non-exempt employees. Being paid a salary is only one part of the exemption test; the job's actual duties must also qualify, and the salary must be above a federal threshold that has changed and been challenged in court in recent years. Check the Department of Labor's overtime page for the current figure rather than assuming a salary alone settles it.

States can go further. California, for example, requires daily overtime after 8 hours in a single day. Federal law has no daily rule and does not require extra pay for weekend or night work by itself.

For this calculator, a salaried worker with three weeks of paid vacation still enters 52 weeks, because the pay continues. An hourly worker who takes two unpaid weeks enters 50. At the $7.25 federal minimum, 40 hours for 52 weeks is $15,080 a year.

Wage and salary questions from job seekers

What is the federal minimum wage?
$7.25 an hour, but many states and cities set higher minimums. Check your state labor department.

How much is $20 an hour a year?
About $41,600 at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks.

What is the difference between biweekly and semi-monthly?
Biweekly is every two weeks (26 checks); semi-monthly is twice a month (24 checks).
