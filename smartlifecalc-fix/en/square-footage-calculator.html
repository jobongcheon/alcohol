Measuring a room the right way

Flooring, carpet, tile and paint are all priced by area, so a careful measurement can save real money. The rule is simple — length times width — but rooms are rarely perfect rectangles, and inches need to be turned into feet first.

How to use the square footage calculator

  1. Measure each room’s length and width in feet.
  2. Enter up to four areas.
  3. Add the price per square foot and a waste allowance if you like.
  4. Press Calculate.

The formula

Square feet = length (ft) × width (ft)  |  1 sq ft = 0.0929 m²

Worked example: two rooms of flooring

A 12 × 14 ft bedroom is 168 sq ft and a 10 × 11 ft office is 110 sq ft, for 278 sq ft in total. With a 10% waste allowance you should buy about 306 sq ft. At $3.49 per sq ft, the material costs about $1,067. The same area is 25.8 m² or 30.9 square yards.

Case study: 278 square feet, sold 19.63 at a time

Flooring is rarely sold by the exact square foot. Laminate, vinyl plank and engineered wood come in cartons, and you can only buy whole ones. Take the two rooms from the example above, 278 sq ft, and a plank whose box label says it covers 19.63 sq ft.

  1. Add 10% waste: 278 × 1.10 = 305.8 sq ft.
  2. Divide by the carton coverage: 305.8 ÷ 19.63 = 15.6 cartons.
  3. Round up, never down: 16 cartons, covering 314.08 sq ft.
  4. Price it by the carton. At $2.99 per sq ft a carton costs $58.69, so 16 cartons come to about $939.

You end up with 36 sq ft more than the bare floor, about 13% extra. That is not wasted money: keep one unopened carton for repairs, because dye lots change. If the plank needs separate underlayment, it usually comes in 100 sq ft rolls, so this job takes three rolls.

Converting inches to feet

Divide the inches by 12 and add them to the feet. A wall that measures 11 ft 7 in is 11 + 7/12 = 11.58 ft. A room of 11 ft 7 in × 13 ft 2 in is 11.58 × 13.17 = about 152.5 sq ft.

L-shaped and odd-shaped rooms

Split the floor into rectangles that do not overlap, measure each one and add them. The calculator has four rows for this. For a closet or bay window, measure it as its own small rectangle.

Paint is measured on the walls

For paint, use the wall area: room perimeter × ceiling height. A 12 × 14 ft room with 8 ft walls has 2 × (12 + 14) × 8 = 416 sq ft of wall. Subtract roughly 20 sq ft for each door and 15 sq ft for each window. A gallon usually covers about 350–400 sq ft per coat, but check the can.

Common mistakes

  • Typing 11.7 for 11 ft 7 in — that is 11 ft 8.4 in. Convert inches first.
  • Forgetting waste and running short of the same dye lot.
  • Using square yards and square feet together: 1 square yard = 9 sq ft.

Why a listing's square footage is not your flooring number

A home listed at 1,500 sq ft will not need 1,500 sq ft of flooring. Listings and mortgage appraisals usually report gross living area under the ANSI Z765 standard, which measures differently from the way you measure a room:

  • It is taken from the outside of the exterior walls. A 30 × 50 ft footprint is 1,500 sq ft outside but about 29 × 49 = 1,421 sq ft inside 6-inch walls, before interior walls and closets come off.
  • Only finished space above grade counts. A finished basement is listed separately, and garages, porches and unfinished attics are left out.
  • Areas with ceilings under about 7 feet are excluded, which trims attic rooms with sloped ceilings.
  • Stairs are counted on the floor they descend from, not on the floor below.

Measured room by room, the floor you can actually cover is often 5 to 10% below the listed figure. Property tax records may use yet another method, so none of these numbers replaces a tape measure.

Reader questions about measuring rooms

How many square feet is a 12 × 12 room?
144 sq ft, or about 13.4 m².

Should I measure wall to wall or include the baseboard?
Measure wall to wall at floor level. The baseboard covers the small gap at the edges.

How do I convert inches to feet?
Divide inches by 12. For example, 10 ft 9 in = 10.75 ft.

How many square feet are in a square yard?
Nine. Carpet is often priced per square yard.
